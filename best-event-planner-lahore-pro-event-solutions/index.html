The 3 Biggest Risks When Hiring an Event Planner in Lahore

Protect your investment and peace of mind by recognizing these standard industry traps:

Risk 01

Pinterest vs. Reality Disasters

Brokers show lavish international Pinterest pictures during sales pitches, then deliver cheap plastic replicas and wilting flowers on event day.

✓ PES Standard: We provide exact 3D CAD renders created specifically for your venue dimensions, followed by live mockups in our workshop.
Risk 02

Lump-Sum "Surprise" Estimates

Opaque single-figure quotes that hide costs until halfway through setup, when planners demand huge cash add-ons for generator fuel, sound, or taxes.

✓ PES Standard: 100% itemized Bill of Quantities (BOQ) detailing stage dimensions, lighting fixtures, generator kVA, and flower stem counts.

Incompetent planners plugging high-load LED walls and warmers into domestic house circuits, tripping power mid-ceremony with no backup.

✓ PES Standard: Dedicated mobile silent diesel generators with automatic transfer switches (ATS) and balanced 3-phase industrial distribution.
Audit Checklist

The 5 Core Criteria to Demand from Any Event Planner

Before signing any contract or paying a deposit in Lahore, verify these 5 operational capabilities:

Criterion 01

1. Do They Own a Dedicated Fabrication Workshop?

Ask for the workshop physical address and visit it. A genuine event production company owns carpentry machinery, welding bays, and paint facilities. If they cannot invite you to their workshop, they are brokers.

Schedule Workshop Visit →
Criterion 02

2. Do They Own Certified AV & Rigging Hardware?

Verify that stage trusses are certified aluminum box trusses with safety cables, not rusted iron poles. Inquire about LED panel pixel pitch (demand P2.5 or P3.9) and sound brands.

Inspect AV Hardware →
Criterion 03

3. Realistic 3D CAD vs. Pinterest

Demand a site-specific 3D architectural plan showing exact stage dimensions, table spacing, and entry arches tailored to your venue.

Request 3D Concept →
Criterion 04

4. Itemized Bill of Quantities (BOQ)

Never accept a single lump sum. Demand unit prices for staging, lighting fixtures, floral varieties, table counts, and generator fuel hours.

Criterion 05

5. Venue Protocols & Society NOCs

Ensure the planner has active experience and operational clearance in gated areas like DHA, Bahria Town, Cantt, and Expo Centre Lahore.

Frequently Asked Questions on Hiring an Event Planner in Lahore

Expert answers to budgeting, booking timelines, and contract terms:

How much does a full-service event planner cost in Lahore? +

Pricing depends on guest count, decor complexity, and technical requirements. Because Pro Event Solutions manufactures staging and decor in our own Rajgarh workshop, we provide direct wholesale fabrication rates with itemized pricing, avoiding the 25-35% middleman broker markup. Contact us for a transparent, line-item quotation tailored to your venue.

How can I verify if an event planner actually owns a workshop? +

Ask to visit their workshop during regular business hours to inspect work-in-progress carpentry, welding, and painting. True production companies will happily welcome you to their facility. We regularly host clients at our Rajgarh workshop in Lahore to review test assemblies of their stages and backdrops.

What backup systems should an event company provide? +

Any professional event company in Pakistan must supply a primary and secondary silent diesel generator with an Automatic Transfer Switch (ATS), redundant audio consoles, spare wireless microphone channels, and an on-site master electrician stationed at the main distribution board throughout the entire event.

How far in advance should I book my event in Lahore? +

For prime wedding season (November through February), we recommend booking 2 to 3 months in advance to secure preferred venue dates and fabrication slots. For corporate conferences and exhibitions, 4 to 6 weeks is standard for 3D design approval, NOC clearances, and pre-production.
