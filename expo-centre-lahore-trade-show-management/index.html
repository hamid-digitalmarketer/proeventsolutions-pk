Turnkey Event Management, Stage Engineering & Creative Production in Lahore

A trade show puts many exhibitors on one floor against one clock. Every stall wants to build at once, every exhibitor wants power and deliveries in the same hour, and the doors open at the printed time regardless of what the aisles look like. Trade show management at Expo Centre Lahore is the work of holding that clock — build schedules, shared services and a floor disciplined enough for business to happen on it.

Coordinating Multiple Exhibitors

The build is a scheduling exercise across dozens of parties. Heavy structures go into the hall first, flooring and electrics follow, and each exhibitor is given a slot they can actually hold — with shared services like power, waste and freight windows allocated so one stall's build never blocks another's access. The schedule is shared, written and enforced, because a polite plan nobody follows is just a wish.

Floor discipline carries through the show itself: aisles kept clear, stalls finished inside the build window, and exhibitor questions answered by a liaison team instead of by whoever is standing nearest. When every exhibitor knows the rules and has a named contact, the floor stays working — and the show looks the way the brochure promised.
