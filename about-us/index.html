Executive Overview: Who We Are & How We Operate

Pro Event Solutions was established to challenge the broken middleman culture in Pakistan's event industry. Rather than acting as commission-taking brokers who subcontract work to unknown third parties, we own our production infrastructure: an active carpentry and metal fabrication workshop in Rajgarh, Lahore, an advanced audiovisual equipment inventory, and a permanent team of CAD designers, electrical engineers, and on-site event supervisors.
