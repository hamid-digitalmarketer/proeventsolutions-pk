Common Pitfalls vs. Our Turnkey Execution

We replace common event headaches with structured, accountable production management:

Shaky, Blurry Mobile Footage

Amateur cameramen shooting with un-stabilized cameras produce jerky, nauseating video with washed-out highlights.

✓ PES Solution: 3-axis motorized Ronin gimbals, dual cinema camera rigs, and 10-bit 4:2:2 high-dynamic-range recording.

Camera-top microphones pick up crowd chatter and room echo while failing to record the speaker's voice clearly.

✓ PES Solution: Direct line feeds from the sound console plus wireless 32-bit float backup lavaliers on speakers.

Endless Delivery Delays

✓ PES Solution: 48-hour social media teaser delivery and guaranteed 21-day final edited master video delivery.
