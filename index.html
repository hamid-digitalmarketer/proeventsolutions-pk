How We Work

The 4-Step Turnkey Execution Blueprint

No surprises, no hidden fees, and zero guesswork. We operate on pre-approved 3D architectural renders and line-item transparency.

Step 01

WhatsApp Discovery & Spatial Brief

Every event begins with a direct, focused conversation on WhatsApp with our lead planning director. We capture your event date, expected guest count, venue preferences, aesthetic style, and technical requirements.

  • Same-day availability check across Lahore venue calendars
  • Site inspection for electrical load, stage clearance & guest flow
Step 02

Photorealistic 3D Concept & Render Approval

We never build on guesswork. Our design team creates scale 3D renders showing the exact stage proportions, LED placement, floral layering, lighting angles, and table centerpieces in your chosen venue.

  • You see and approve the exact visual design before fabrication begins
  • Revisions and adjustments made in 3D without expensive on-site mistakes
Step 03

Line-Item Costing & Scope Confirmation

No arbitrary "silver, gold, platinum" bundles that force you into items you do not need. We present an itemized line-by-line quotation detailing the cost of stage carpentry, audio, trussing, florals, and catering.

  • Full budget transparency with flexible scope customization
  • Contractual price lock — zero surprise additions on event day
Step 04

Turnkey Execution & On-Site Direction

On the day of your event, a dedicated Senior Event Director and technical crew manage load-in, fabrication, sound testing, cue-to-cue run-of-show, guest flow, and load-out so you can host with total peace of mind.

  • Live stage management, audio monitoring & backup power systems
  • Catering coordination ensuring dinner serves hot exactly on schedule
