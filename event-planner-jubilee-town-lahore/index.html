Lawn, Hall and Marquee Spaces Around Jubilee Town

Working Notes for Jubilee Town Functions

Lawn capacity is the first honest number in any plan here — a lawn that seats ninety comfortably will not stretch to one hundred and fifty, whatever the furniture list promises. Power comes next: lighting, sound and cooling loads on a domestic supply need a distribution check before the build, not after. Parking finishes the trio, because planned-society streets stay calm only until a function fills them, and guest cars need a set pattern so neighbours keep their access.
