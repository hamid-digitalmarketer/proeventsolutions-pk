Wedding Functions We Manage

Each function is designed on its own terms, then tied to the rest of the sequence through a shared palette and a shared plan.

Mehndi Function Decor

Mehndi is the loudest night of the sequence, and the decor should match. We build marigold installations in fresh or artificial bloom, drapery in bright jewel-toned palettes, and a dedicated dholki corner with seating arranged around the performance area. Backdrops stay photo-friendly, because this is the night the candid cameras never stop.

Mayun Decoration

Mayun is traditionally an intimate function — often at home — and we design it that way. Yellow and gold florals, a raised seating area for the bride, and lighting that flatters an evening of family routines rather than a stage show. The practical side still gets full attention: power distribution, service space and guest seating are planned like any larger event.

Barat Coordination

The Barat carries the heaviest logistics of the wedding. We design and build the main stage, plan the groom's entry and procession, and manage guest flow from parking to dining to stage queue. Sound, lighting and catering timings are coordinated under the same plan, and a dedicated supervisor stays on-site through the night.

Walima Reception Design

The Walima asks for a calmer, more formal mood. We design a refined stage with soft ambient lighting, style the dining layout so queues keep moving, and coordinate service with the catering team so dinner starts on schedule even after a long photo session.

Engagement Ceremonies

Engagements are compact by design — a couple-focused stage, a clear point for the ring exchange, a clean backdrop for photographs and seating for the close circle. When the engagement opens a longer sequence, we carry its palette into the functions that follow so the whole wedding reads as one story.

Wedding Stage Design & Decor

The stage anchors every function, so we treat it as a design project rather than a rental package. Backdrops are custom-built to the venue's dimensions and layered with florals, tonal drapery and statement lighting, with aisle, seating and photo-corner styling drawn from the same palette. For couples who want a stronger statement, options such as a luxury floral stage or a themed bridal entry — a floral tunnel or a doli reveal — are designed into the concept from the first draft.

Nothing is fabricated on guesswork. You receive 3D stage renders and floral schematics, approve them, and the approved design is what gets built on site. The carpentry and fabrication crew behind this work also builds exhibition environments at Expo Centre Lahore — see our event production for Expo Centre Lahore page — so large structural stages are a familiar brief rather than an experiment.

Choosing Your Wedding Season & Dates

Lahore's peak wedding season runs from October to March, and the most wanted dates — weekends from late November through February — go first. Venue availability tightens months ahead of those dates, so families who already have a preferred hall, marquee or farmhouse should lock the date early rather than settle for what is left. Off-peak dates and weekday functions are easier to book and often allow more build time for custom decor. If you are still deciding between venues in DHA , Gulberg or around Johar Town , send us the shortlist and we will help you weigh each option against your guest count.

Starting Points, Not Fixed Packages

Weddings vary too much for fixed bundles, so we quote by scope. Three starting points cover most briefs:

  • Single-function styling — one event, such as a Mehndi, Mayun or engagement, with stage and backdrop design, florals and on-site supervision. Full wedding sequence — Mehndi, Barat and Walima planned under one design theme and one timeline, with shared vendors and a single point of contact from first function to last. Large-scale custom builds — multi-function weddings at large venues, with custom stage architecture, extended floral and lighting design and full guest-flow planning.

Whichever starting point fits, the estimate stays itemized and the design stays 3D-approved before build. Catering and venue logistics can be folded into the same plan — see catering & venue management .
