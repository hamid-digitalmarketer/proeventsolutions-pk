Park View City sits off Multan Road in southern Lahore, part of the wave of planned societies that has grown up along that corridor. It is a young, family-heavy address — generous homes, growing lawns — and its events follow the same pattern: mehndis and nikkahs at home, bigger barats and walimas on the lawn, and the largest nights at a marquee on the road out.

Being off Multan Road means every event starts with arrival planning. Guests come in through Thokar Niaz Baig or along Multan Road itself, both heavy at weekend evenings, so invitations, arrival notes and parking plans are built into the programme from the first drawing rather than added as an afterthought.

Families settled here recently also plan housewarmings and smaller get-togethers alongside the weddings, and those lighter formats sit comfortably within the same service scope.

The society's open plots and lawns give plenty of room to build; the work is in matching the scale to the street width, the power supply and the season. Venues and Practical Notes in Park View City

The society itself offers lawns, open plots and function grounds; the named venues begin along Multan Road, where Delite Marquee and Bel Avenir carry the larger nights, and continue towards the Canal side through Thokar Niaz Baig. For most families the choice is straightforward: build at home for functions up to a few hundred guests, take a marquee on the corridor for the rest.

The practical notes are about the street, not the hall. Society lanes are quiet but narrow, so load-ins are staggered and parking is marshalled at the nearest corner.
