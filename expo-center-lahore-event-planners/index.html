Common Exhibition Pitfalls vs. Our Turnkey Execution

We replace chaotic contractor delays with structured, accountable production management:

Pitfall 01

Chaotic Move-In & Missed Deadlines

Third-party contractors building entirely on-site cause chaotic traffic, safety violations, and incomplete booths at opening bell.

✓ PES Solution: 80% pre-fabrication in our Rajgarh workshop ensures rapid 12-hour on-site assembly with zero frantic rush.
Pitfall 02

Dangerous Power Surges & Tripping

Overloaded electrical junctions and loose floor cabling cause tripping hazards and fry expensive display equipment.

✓ PES Solution: Industrial 3-phase distribution boards (DBs), balanced phase loads, and heavy-duty rubber cable ramps across all aisles.
Pitfall 03

Rigging & Structural Instability

Substandard aluminum trusses or uncalculated hanging point loads pose serious safety hazards inside high-ceiling halls.

✓ PES Solution: Structural box trussing with certified load calculation, safety cables, and secondary tethering.

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to Common Expo Planning Questions

Everything you need to know about exhibition logistics, permissions, and stall fabrication:

What are the official ceiling height limits for stalls at Expo Centre Lahore? +

Standard shell scheme booths are limited to 2.44m (8ft). Custom wooden stalls can typically build up to 4.5m to 5m depending on the hall zone, with proper structural engineering approval from Expo Centre management.

Do you handle Expo Centre NOCs and electrical load permissions? +

Yes. We manage all technical drawings, load sanction applications, fire safety certifications, and contractor security passes required by Expo Centre administration.

How far in advance should we book exhibition stall fabrication? +

For standard custom stalls (18sqm to 72sqm), we recommend confirming 3 to 4 weeks prior to move-in. For large pavilions (100sqm+), 4 to 6 weeks allows optimal 3D refinement and workshop pre-assembly.

Can you provide 24/7 on-site standby support during the exhibition? +

Yes. Every client is assigned a dedicated on-site standby team including an electrical technician, touch-up painter, and project coordinator throughout all show hours.
