Cracked Cyc Wall Curves

Improper plastering and weak wood framing cause cyc curves to crack when models or props place weight on them.

✓ PES Solution: High-density structural rib framing with multi-ply curved marine plywood and commercial floor sealers.

Heavy Lighting Floor Clutter

C-stands, sandbags, and cables across the studio floor create tripping hazards and slow down camera movements.

✓ PES Solution: Ceiling-mounted overhead pipe lighting grid with spring-loaded pantographs and concealed power outlets.

Inconsistent Color Temperature

Mixing fluorescent ceiling lights with professional strobes creates green color casts that ruin post-production.

✓ PES Solution: Certified 98+ CRI bi-color LED studio panels with DMX console control.
