What We Build

Infinity Cyclorama Curve Walls

The cyclorama — "cyc" — is the curved backdrop where the floor bends into the wall without a corner, giving product, fashion and portrait work an endless background. We fabricate single-wall, L-shaped and U-shaped curves framed to the room and finished in an ultra-matte coat that keeps the curve unbroken on camera and easy to touch up.

Motorized Overhead Pantograph Lighting Grids

Pantographs are scissor-like arms hung from ceiling rails that carry studio lights: each light raises, lowers and swings into position without a single stand on the floor. The grid keeps the shooting area clear and makes turnaround between setups a matter of seconds.

Commercial Shooting Bays

Dedicated bays for tabletop and e-commerce work, with shooting tables, diffused overhead light positions and clean sightlines for product and macro shots.

Prep & Makeup Corners

Illuminated vanity and styling corners where models and clients prepare, separated from the shooting area so the set stays clear while the next look gets ready.

Storage & Backdrop Systems

Backdrop rollers, shelving and equipment storage built into the design, so gear lives in the studio instead of traveling in and out of every shoot.

Cyc Wall & Lighting Explained

A cyclorama solves a simple problem: normal rooms have corners, and corners show in the frame. The curve makes wall and floor one continuous surface, so the background reads as endless — white or any color you paint it — with no edge to retouch out of every shot.

Overhead pantograph grids matter for the same reason from the other direction. Light stands cluster around the subject, creep into wide shots and slow every setup change; hanging the lights from the ceiling clears the floor and lets you re-light without moving a single tripod.

Build Process

  • Measure. Room dimensions and ceiling height are recorded first, because they decide the cyc radius, the grid layout and what the space can honestly support. Structural and electrical checks. Lights are balanced and the cyc is shot under working conditions, and the studio is signed off with a test shoot rather than a promise.
