Common Exhibition Pitfalls vs. Our Turnkey Execution

Pitfall 01

Scuffed Flooring & Dusty Displays

Thousands of walking visitors track dust and scuff glossy booth floors within the first few hours.

✓ PES Solution: Protective floor film during move-in, plus dedicated midday touch-up cleaners and overnight deep-detailing.

Display screens crashing or spotlights burning out leave your stall looking neglected and unprofessional.

✓ PES Solution: On-site standby electrician with backup LED spotlights, replacement HDMI cables, and media players.

✓ PES Solution: Professional corporate hospitality hostesses managing the coffee bar, executive snacks, and business card logging.
