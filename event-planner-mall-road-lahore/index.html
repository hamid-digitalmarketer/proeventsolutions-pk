Event Venues in and Around Mall Road

Popular venues in and around Mall Road include Avari Lahore, whose Khorshed Mahal ballroom and Indus Hall cover formats from formal dinners to mid-size institutional gatherings. Beyond the hotel, the corridor's older institutions, club spaces and Cantt-side banquet rooms host member and family functions under their own house rules.

Institutional venues come with protocol: guest lists checked at gates, vehicles registered in advance, and timings that respect the neighbourhood's official character. We handle those requirements as part of the plan rather than as exceptions discovered on the night.

Planning Notes for Mall Road Events

Security desks and vehicle gates decide the pace of arrivals on this corridor, so guest lists, car numbers and vendor credentials are submitted well ahead of the event. Load-ins follow the hotel's window, because ballroom floors and service lifts are shared resources and our crews work inside the slots the venue assigns.

Traffic along the Mall is steady rather than extreme, but protocol convoys and procession closures can seal a lane without warning, so the run sheet carries buffer time on every arrival stage. For evening functions, guest drop-off points are agreed with the venue so the frontage never congests.
