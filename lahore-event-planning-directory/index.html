Frequently Asked Questions

Lahore Locality & Venue Operations FAQs

Important guidelines regarding society permissions, security, and timings:

Do you manage DHA and Cantt security gate passes for setup labor? +

Yes. All our full-time production crew carry verified computerized CNICs, police verification records, and company uniforms. We handle all formal gate clearance submissions with DHA security offices and Cantonment Board administration.

How do you handle sound curfews in residential societies? +

Most residential societies in Lahore enforce outdoor sound restrictions at 10:00 PM. We deploy directional acoustic speakers focused inwards towards the dance floor/lounge, minimizing spill into neighboring houses, and coordinate transitions into indoor lounges when curfews arrive.

Can you set up temporary German marquees on private home lawns? +

Yes. We erect custom-dimensioned aluminum clear-span marquees directly over 1-kanal, 2-kanal, and 4-kanal residential lawns with grass-protection subfloors, integrated heating/cooling units, and chandelier lighting.
