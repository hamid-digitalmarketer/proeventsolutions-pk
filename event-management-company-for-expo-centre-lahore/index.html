Expo Centre Lahore at a Glance

The complex sits in Johar Town, near Emporium Mall and the Nishat Hotel, which makes it a straightforward trip for exhibitors and visitors coming from anywhere in Lahore. What is inside the boundary wall decides how an event should be planned:

  • Exhibition Halls 1–3 , plus a 4th hall in active use — the main spaces for exhibitions and trade shows. A Convention Centre with auditoriums, a foyer and meeting rooms — where conferences, seminars and breakout sessions run alongside or instead of hall events. An open event area — outdoor space used for formats that do not belong under a roof.

Which hall or room you get matters more than most first-time exhibitors expect. Footfall, aisle position, neighbouring stalls and the distance from the entrance all change how a stall should be designed, staffed and budgeted. We assess the specific hall and spot before design begins, so the plan matches the room it will actually stand in.

Exhibitor Planning & Budgeting Guide

An honest budget starts with the factors that move it, not a number copied from a rate card. For a stall or a show at Expo Centre Lahore, six factors do most of the work:

  • Hall choice. Position inside the complex changes footfall and how hard a stall has to work to be seen, which flows into design and staffing. Booth size and build complexity. Floor area is only the start. Custom heights, curved walls, flooring and storage all add material and crew hours. AV and LED. Screens, sound and lighting are scoped by equipment, count and crew days. Graphics. Printed area and finishing quality — a wall meant to be read from three metres is a different job from one read from thirty. Install and dismantle labour. Crew size, and how many days the build window and the dismantle slot actually allow. Show duration. Every extra show day adds staffing, stall care and consumables.

Send those six answers on WhatsApp — hall, spot, size, build ambition, AV needs and show dates — and we will return an itemized estimate, line by line, so you can see what each decision costs before committing to it.

Our Expo Centre Process

  • Site and hall assessment. We study the booked hall and spot — access, neighbours, footfall — before design starts. Design and 3D approval. The concept and 3D renders are shared and approved by you before anything is fabricated. Off-site fabrication. Stalls and stage elements are built away from the venue, so hall time is spent on assembly, not carpentry. Install within the build window. Crew and materials arrive in the sequence the window allows, with the supervisor holding the schedule. Show-time staffing. The supervisor and floor crew keep the stall or the show working through every show day. Dismantle and handover. The stand comes down safely, on schedule, and the space is handed back as the venue expects.
