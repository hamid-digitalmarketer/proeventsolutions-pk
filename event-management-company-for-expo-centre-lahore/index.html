Executive Overview: Expo Centre Lahore Turnkey Operations

Expo Centre Lahore is Pakistan's premier venue for international industrial expos, medical congresses, educational summits, and trade fairs. Managing an event at this Johar Town complex requires specialized knowledge of hall load-in regulations, security clearances, three-phase power balancing, and fire safety protocols. Pro Event Solutions serves as your on-ground operational partner, providing in-house fabrication, registration gantries, stage production, and complete trade stall setup.
