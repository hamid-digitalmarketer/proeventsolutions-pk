Function Halls and Event Spaces Near LDA Avenue

The hall options around the scheme fall into three types: enclosed banquet halls on the main approach roads, marquee structures on the open southern approaches, and large home lawns for the smaller guest lists. As a planned LDA scheme, the avenues run wide, which helps vendor movement and guest parking more than most older parts of Lahore. LDA Avenue Event Planning Notes

Hall bookings set the pace. Once a venue is confirmed, its ceiling height, stage depth and kitchen rules decide what the design can be — which is why we ask for venue details before quoting decor.
