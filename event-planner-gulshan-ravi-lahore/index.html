Outdoor Planning Notes for Gulshan-e-Ravi Functions

Weather is the first conversation. The dependable stretch runs from autumn through spring; summer evenings work with shaded seating and later starts; and monsoon weeks call for covered structures or a hall fallback booked alongside the lawn. That decision is put in front of hosts early, while every option is still open.

Light is the second conversation. Outdoor evenings live or die on lighting design — warm washes across the dining area, focused light on the stage, dimmed paths along the garden edges — and all of it draws power that must be distributed across the ground. Handled properly, the lawn at night becomes the strongest room at the function.
