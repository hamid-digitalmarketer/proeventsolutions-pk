Expo Centre and Nearby Banquet Venues

Popular venues in and around Johar Town include Expo Centre Lahore on Abdul Haque Road, The Nishat Hotel Johar Town and Topaz Marquee. The Expo Centre itself runs Exhibition Halls 1–3 plus a fourth hall in active use, a Convention Centre with auditoriums, a foyer and meeting rooms, and open event areas — the practical home for trade shows, seminars and product launches. The Nishat and Topaz cover the hotel-ballroom and marquee formats that weddings and corporate dinners need.

Two notes for planners. First, exhibition move-ins at the Expo follow the organizers' schedules, so side events — client dinners, team dinners, launch parties — need call times that respect those windows. Second, Johar Town's banquet density means weekend dates in season go early; halls within a kilometre of each other compete for the same Saturdays, so confirming the venue first and the decor second is the sequence that works.

Johar Town Event Logistics

Access is easy — the area plugs into Canal Road, the Emporium side and the Kalma crossing — but parking is where events are won or lost. Banquet halls share streets with the food-street rush, so guest parking plans, valet points and vendor unloading zones are agreed before the design is finalised. For Expo-linked work, freight and stall materials move on the Centre's own schedules, and our fabrication crews are used to building to those windows.

Residential pockets between the commercial strips keep us honest on sound: amplified audio stays within venue rules and reasonable hours, and late-night pack-downs are run quietly where homes back onto service lanes.
