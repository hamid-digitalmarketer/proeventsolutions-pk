Working With Corporate Procurement

Corporate events are approved internally before they happen, so we write for that process. The estimate is itemized line by line — materials, crew days, AV, catering, dismantle — so it can circulate for sign-off without translation, and the written scope states what is included rather than what is assumed.

Approvals continue through the project at fixed checkpoints: 3D renders before fabrication, the run-of-show before doors open, and any change costed in writing before it is made. That documentation habit is not bureaucracy for its own sake; it is what lets a procurement team approve quickly and a finance team close the file without questions afterwards.
