Common Exhibition Pitfalls vs. Our Turnkey Execution

We replace chaotic contractor delays with structured, accountable production management:

Pitfall 01

Unsafe Overhead Truss Rigging

Hanging heavy lighting and LED screens without certified load calculations risks catastrophic structural failure.

✓ PES Solution: Certified heavy-duty aluminum box trusses, secondary safety steel lanyards, and motorized chain hoists rated for heavy commercial loads.
Pitfall 02

Flickering LED Screens on Live Camera

Low-refresh-rate LED panels create scan lines and banding on broadcast television and photography.

✓ PES Solution: High-refresh-rate (3840Hz+) indoor P2.5 and P3.9 LED panels driven by high-end Novastar 4K processors.
Pitfall 03

Muffled Sound in Cavernous Spaces

Inadequate speaker dispersion results in echoey, unintelligible audio across large hall seating areas.

✓ PES Solution: Acoustically modeled line-array clusters with supplementary delay towers ensuring uniform frequency coverage from front to back.

How It Works

The 5-Step Expo Centre Delivery Workflow

Precision technical production workflow:

01

Technical Rider & Power Audit

We evaluate your audiovisual rider, input channel lists, video formats, and total electrical wattage requirements.

02

CAD Truss Rigging & Sightline Plot

Plotting load-bearing truss points, speaker angles, and camera positions in 3D to ensure unobstructed view lines.

03

Equipment Testing & Staging Prep

Bench-testing LED tiles, checking cable looms, and loading media content at our equipment facility.

04

On-Site Rigging & Calibration

Erecting truss structures, flying line-arrays, calibrating audio time-alignment, and color-matching LED screens.

05

Live Show Calling & Redundant Control

Lead technical director operating redundant video switchers and audio consoles with seamless backup.
