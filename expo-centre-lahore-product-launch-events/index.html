Coordinating With Your Trade Show Presence

If you are already exhibiting at a show in the halls, launching beside your own stall multiplies both. The launch borrows the show's traffic; the stall inherits the launch's spotlight for the rest of the exhibition. We coordinate the two so the reveal moment pushes visitors into a demo zone that keeps working long after the speeches end — and the stall itself is designed to catch the flow, which is the brief our exhibition stall design at Expo Centre Lahore team works to.

Launch-Day Choreography

The day runs on a cue sheet: guest arrival and registration, seating, the countdown, the reveal, the keynote or demonstration, the press moment, then the demo zone opening. A supervisor holds the cues with the stage, LED and sound crews, so each transition happens on the sheet rather than on feel.

Filming is planned with the choreography, not bolted on. Multi-camera 4K videography captures the reveal from more than one angle for the projected live feed and the edited record afterwards — options are on our videography services page. When the launch closes, the same team manages the pack-down so the hall is handed back on schedule.
