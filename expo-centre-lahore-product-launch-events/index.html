Common Exhibition Pitfalls vs. Our Turnkey Execution

We replace chaotic contractor delays with structured, accountable production management:

Pitfall 01

Anti-Climactic or Malfunctioning Reveal

Curtains getting stuck or reveal mechanics lagging destroys the emotional climax of months of marketing buildup.

✓ PES Solution: Precision-engineered electronic DMX Kabuki drop systems and automated turntable mechanics tested in 10+ dry runs.
Pitfall 02

Inadequate Power for Heavy Staging & Lighting

High-power moving head lighting and audio systems drawing sudden current spikes can blow hall breakers during the reveal.

✓ PES Solution: Dedicated silent generator power isolated exclusively for stage lighting, pyro, and reveal mechanics.

Poor Media & VIP Sightlines

Photographers and journalists unable to capture clean hero shots of the newly revealed product.

✓ PES Solution: Multi-tiered press risers with direct SDI video mult-boxes and dedicated studio-grade key lighting on the product.
